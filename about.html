About Tiny Neurons

Pioneering the future of artificial intelligence through innovative research and collaboration

Our Story

From humble beginnings to cutting-edge research

Founded in 2013, Tiny Neurons Research Group began as a small team of passionate researchers united by a shared vision: to push the boundaries of artificial intelligence and make meaningful contributions to science and society.

What started as late-night discussions about neural networks and machine learning algorithms has evolved into a world-class research facility that attracts brilliant minds from around the globe. Our journey has been marked by groundbreaking discoveries, innovative methodologies, and a commitment to open science.

Today, we stand at the forefront of AI research, tackling some of the most challenging problems in computer vision, natural language processing, and deep learning. Our work spans from theoretical foundations to practical applications that benefit healthcare, education, and society as a whole.
