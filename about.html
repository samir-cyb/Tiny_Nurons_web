About Tiny Neurons

A group of enthusiasts is currently working on AI, ML, TinyML, LLM, IoT, Operating Systems, Computer Operations and Applications, Computer Vision, Renewable Energy, Semiconductors, Energy Management, Blockchain, and Cryptography. The research team finds, polishes, and develops ideas while analyzing their social impact.

How Journey Started

From humble beginnings to cutting-edge research

The Journey of the Tiny Neurons Research Group started in 2023 from a late-night conversation among a group of enthusiasts who were interested in doing research on neural networks and machine learning algorithms. They built a team and pushed the boundaries of their knowledge in AI, determined to make meaningful contributions to science and society.

In recent days, this team, standing at the forefront of AI research, is tackling some of the most challenging problems in computer vision, natural language processing (NLP), deep learning (DL), and many more. Their work spans from theoretical foundations to practical applications that benefit healthcare, education, and society as a whole.

Our Research Philosophy
